# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free learning and research community.
Contributors should be respectful, assume good faith, give constructive technical
feedback, and make space for beginners. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior is harmful to the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the
maintainer contact method listed on the GitHub repository. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
